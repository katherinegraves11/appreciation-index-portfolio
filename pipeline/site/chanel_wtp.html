What a Chanel bag is worth the moment you leave the boutique

Chanel has raised prices every year since 2020. The secondhand market hasn't kept up, except for one bag. We looked at 483 listings on The RealReal and 2,074 more across four other resale sites, then lined every bag up against the price Chanel charges for it new, and against the designer who created it.

How each era is netting out

Coco Chanel
Designs from 1955
26¢

Her 2.55 is the oldest stock on resale, and it shows. Vintage reissues with real wear sell for about a quarter of the estimated retail. Heritage is not the same as value retention.

Karl Lagerfeld
1983 – 2019
32–80¢

The widest spread. His icons hold the middle: Classic Flap 68¢, Chanel 19 50¢. His trend bags are where the money went, with the Gabrielle at 32¢ and the Boy at 40¢ against RealReal's estimate.

Virginie Viard
2019 – 2024
78¢–$1.10

Critics were cool on Viard. Resale buyers are not. The Chanel 22 holds 78¢ and the Chanel 25 asks $1.10 on the dollar, above the boutique price.

His first bags are barely on resale yet, so this is a first sighting, not a verdict. What's there asks the highest prices on the board, about $8,800 on average, and gets three times the saves of an average Chanel listing.

Why the older bags look worse

Chanel moved the goalposts

A medium Classic Flap cost $7,800 in 2021. Today it is $11,700. Resale asks have not climbed that fast, so a bag bought in 2021 and sold today roughly breaks even, while the same bag bought this year would not.

How to read this

Five words, no jargon

Retail
What Chanel charges in the boutique today, in US dollars.
Resale ask
The price a seller is listing the bag for secondhand. Not what it finally sold for; sale prices come later as we track listings over time.
Cents on the dollar
Resale ask divided by retail. 68¢ means a $100 bag is listed at $68.
Estimated retail
The RealReal's own guess at the original price, printed on about one listing in five. We use it only when we don't have the boutique price for that size.
Small sample
Fewer than three listings. Interesting, not conclusive. The number will firm up as the index reads more pages each day.
